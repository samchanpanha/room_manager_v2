# Part 1 — System Overview

## 1.1 System name

The application is called **RentManager** (package name `rentmanager`). It is a
rental and co-living **property operations platform** for landlords, property
managers and their teams.

It runs as a web application (Next.js). Staff use it in a desktop browser
(back office); tenants and owners use it on a phone via the **Tenant Portal**
and **Owner Portal**. There is also a **Telegram bot** for notifications and
quick questions.

## 1.2 Purpose — what the system manages

RentManager runs a rental / co-living property business **end to end**, from
the physical building to the owner's monthly payout:

- **Property inventory** — properties → buildings → floors → rooms → beds.
- **People** — members (tenants/residents), owners (landlords), staff users, vendors.
- **Contracts** — member leases (occupancy contracts) and owner contracts (management agreements).
- **Billing** — automatic monthly rent, services and utility invoices generated by a rent engine.
- **Collection** — payments by cash, bank transfer, QR, card or cheque, with numbered receipts.
- **Deposits** — security deposits billed, held, deducted and refunded.
- **Operations** — room moves, inspections, maintenance tickets, complaints, staff attendance.
- **Store** — a point-of-sale (POS) counter and stock/inventory control.
- **Finances** — an immutable double-entry ledger, expenses, budgets, Profit & Loss, owner statements.
- **Self-service** — a tenant portal (pay rent, raise tickets, upload documents) and an owner portal.
- **Notifications** — in-app, dashboard alerts and a Telegram bot (receipts, reminders, statements).
- **Governance** — role-based permissions on every page and API call, audit log, 2FA, reports.

### Who uses it

| User | Where they work | What they do |
|---|---|---|
| Super Admin / Admin | Admin console | Configure org, users, roles, settings, security |
| Property Manager | Admin console | Run assigned properties: rooms, leases, operations, approvals |
| Accountant | Admin console | Rent engine, invoices, payments, deposits, ledger, owner statements |
| Staff / cashier / front desk | Admin console (scoped) | Register members, leases, bill, collect payment, POS, tickets, attendance |
| Owner (landlord) | Owner portal | View own buildings, occupancy, statements & documents (read-only) |
| Member (tenant) | Tenant portal + Telegram | View invoices, pay by QR, raise requests, upload KYC, deposit status |

### Business problems it solves

- **Nothing falls through the cracks** — rooms, leases, invoices and tickets all have states and histories.
- **Rent is billed automatically and correctly** — proration for mid-month moves, late fees, dunning reminders.
- **Money is always traceable** — every invoice, payment, refund and expense posts balanced double-entry lines; nothing posted can be deleted.
- **Collections are easy for tenants** — pay-by-QR, tenant portal, Telegram receipts.
- **Owners get a fair monthly statement** — collected rent × share (or fixed rent), minus fees and costs.
- **The right people see the right things** — staff are limited to their properties; members and owners see only their own records.

## 1.4 How the system works — the end-to-end lifecycle

Information flows in one direction: **the physical world → contracts → billing →
money → accounting → reports → owners**.

```
 Sign in
   │
   ▼
 Dashboard (occupancy, collected vs billed, arrears, cash, alerts)
   │
   ▼
 Property → Building → Floor → Room (set price & status)        [M04]
   │
   ▼
 Owner + Owner Contract (fixed rent OR revenue share %)         [M03, M05]
   │
   ▼
 Member profile + KYC documents (prospect → verified)           [M02, M17]
   │
   ▼
 Lease on a room (draft → active)                               [M05]
   │        ├── room becomes "occupied", member becomes "active"
   │        ├── deposit billed (installment invoices)           [M10]
   │        └── services assigned (WiFi/parking/laundry)        [M12]
   │
   ▼
 Monthly billing job runs                                       [M06, M07]
   │        rent + services + utilities (meters) + one-offs
   │        − discounts + tax + late fees  =  Invoice (issued, gapless number, PDF)
   │
   ▼
 Member pays (QR in portal / cash / bank / card / cheque)       [M09, M13]
   │        ├── receipt PDF auto-filed (RCP-… )
   │        ├── invoice flips to partial_paid → paid
   │        └── overpayment stays as member credit
   │
   ▼
 Ledger posts balanced double-entry lines (append-only)         [M08]
   │
   ├── Expenses recorded (vendor costs, parts, maintenance)     [M20, M15, M19]
   ├── POS sales & stock movements                              [M14, M15]
   │
   ▼
 Profit & Loss report (revenue − expenses)                      [M26]
   │
   ▼
 Owner Statement generated → approved → paid                    [M24]
   │        collected × share | fixed rent − fees − costs = net payout
   │
   ▼
 Reports, dashboard KPIs, Telegram notifications                [M26, M21, M33]
```

**The golden rule:** nothing financial is ever deleted. An issued invoice is
corrected with a **credit note**; a wrong ledger entry is undone with a
**reversal**; a paid expense is **voided** (which reverses it). This keeps the
books balanced and the full history auditable.

## 1.5 Key relationships between modules

- A **Property** has many **Buildings**; a building has many **Floors**; a floor
  has many **Rooms**; a room can have many **Beds**. Almost everything else
  hangs off the property/room.
- A **Building** belongs to one **Owner**, formalised by one open **Owner Contract** (`M05`).
- A **Member** signs a **Lease** for a room/bed. Activating the lease changes
  the room status to *occupied* and the member to *active*.
- A **Lease** drives **Invoices** (via the rent engine), carries a **Deposit**
  and can have **Services** and **Utility meters** attached.
- **Invoices** are paid by **Payments**, which are allocated to invoices
  (oldest-first). Both post to the **Ledger**.
- **Deposits** sit in a liability account (they are *not* revenue) until refunded
  or deducted at move-out.
- **Maintenance tickets**, **inspections** and **POS/stock** all feed costs into
  **Expenses** and the **P&L**; owner-borne costs flow into **Owner Statements**.
- Every record is **property-scoped**, and every mutation writes an **Audit log** row.
